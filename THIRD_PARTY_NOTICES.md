## Price-match research and public data sources

The product concepts in [awsdataarchitect/costco-price-match](https://github.com/awsdataarchitect/costco-price-match) were reviewed as design research. That repository did not include a license when reviewed, so its source code was not incorporated. Costco Receipt Organizer's local deal scanner and matching implementation are independent.

CocoWest and CocoEast are independent Costco fan sites used for Canadian sale listings. U.S. sale data is read from Costco's public Warehouse Savings page. The app links to the source page for every deal and does not imply affiliation with or endorsement by Costco Wholesale, CocoWest, or CocoEast.
